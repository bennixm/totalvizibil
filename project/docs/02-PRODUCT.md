# Product Specification

## Main Users

### Business
A local company that wants an online presence, customers, traffic, marketing, and simple management.

### Customer
A person looking for a local service.

### Platform Admin
The operator of the marketplace.

## Product Areas

### Business
- AI onboarding
- Website
- Campaigns
- Credit
- Analytics
- AI assistant

### Customer
- Search
- Business profiles
- Results
- Contact actions
- Optional AI search

### Admin
- Businesses
- Traffic
- Campaigns
- Financial data
- AI costs
- Ranking
- Platform performance

## Core Product Principle

The interface should remain simple even if the backend is complex.
