# Campaign Management

## Campaign Lifecycle

Draft → Ready → Active → Paused → Exhausted → Completed

## Campaign Components

- target locations
- services
- budget
- traffic source
- targeting
- tracking
- performance

## External Traffic

Google Ads can be used to acquire external traffic.

## Internal Monetization

Traffic can generate billable interactions with businesses.

## Important

Campaign spending must be tracked independently from client credit.
