# Business AI Onboarding

## Goal

Allow a business to go from zero to active without requiring technical knowledge.

## Experience

The business enters the platform.

An AI agent appears as a live conversational assistant.

The interaction should feel similar to a video call. The user's camera is not required.

## Flow

1. AI greeting
2. Business discovery
3. Services
4. Locations
5. Contact information
6. Website preview
7. Preview feedback
8. Account creation
9. Payment
10. Promotional credit
11. Production website
12. Campaign configuration
13. Activation

## Resource Control

Pre-payment AI usage must be limited.

Example limits:
- maximum conversation duration
- maximum preview generations
- maximum AI cost per prospect

## Activation

Expensive production resources should be activated after payment confirmation.
