# Website Builder

## Goal

Generate a professional website without requiring the business owner to know how to build websites.

## Template System

Use predefined templates by industry.

Examples:
- Roofing
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- Construction
- Cleaning
- Automotive
- Restaurants

## AI Responsibilities

AI can generate:
- headlines
- descriptions
- service text
- calls to action
- business summaries
- SEO content

## Preview

Before activation:
- temporary preview
- limited generation
- limited modifications
- low resource consumption

## Production

After activation:
- production website
- analytics
- tracking
- permanent content
- domain/subdomain
- SEO
