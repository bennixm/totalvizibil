# Ranking Engine

## Goal

Show the most useful businesses to customers while maintaining a healthy economic model for the platform.

## Ranking Inputs

Potential inputs:
- relevance
- business quality
- historical performance
- customer outcomes
- economic value
- availability
- exploration

## Initial Example

Priority Score:

30% Relevance
25% Performance
20% Economic Value
15% Quality
10% Exploration

These percentages are experimental and must be validated.

## Important Rule

Highest payment must not automatically mean highest position.

A business must remain relevant to the customer's request.

## Exploration

New and smaller businesses should receive controlled exposure so the platform can discover high-performing businesses.
