# Customer Search

## Primary Search

Classic search is the default.

Example:

"roof repair Cluj"

The search engine converts this into structured parameters.

## AI Search

AI search is optional.

Example:

"I have water coming through my roof and need someone urgently in Cluj."

AI extracts:
- service
- category
- location
- urgency

The structured query is passed to the normal search engine.

## Important Architecture Principle

AI interprets the request.

The search engine performs the search.

The AI should not replace the search engine.
