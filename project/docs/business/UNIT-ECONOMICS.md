# Unit Economics

## Core Metrics

### CAC
Customer Acquisition Cost: cost required to acquire an activated business.

### LTV
Lifetime Value of a business: total contribution generated by a business during its lifetime.

### Revenue Per Business
Total recognized revenue generated by a business.

### Google Cost
Cost of external traffic acquisition.

### AI Cost
AI and voice cost attributable to the business.

## Core Formula

Contribution Margin =
Revenue
- Google Cost
- Stripe Fees
- AI Costs
- Infrastructure Costs
- Other Variable Costs

## Important Metrics

- AI Cost / Prospect
- AI Cost / Activated Business
- CAC
- Average Initial Credit
- Average Monthly Credit Consumption
- Revenue / Business
- Google Cost / Business
- Contribution Margin / Business
- LTV
- LTV / CAC

## Validation Required

Do not assume final values for:
- CPC
- initial credit
- conversion rate
- AI cost
- average business spend
- margin

These must be measured with real users.
