# Business Model

## Primary Revenue Model

Businesses purchase promotional credit.

The credit is consumed when eligible promotional services are delivered.

## Initial Credit

Potential starting options:
- 100 RON
- 250 RON
- 500 RON
- 1000 RON

Final amounts must be validated through testing.

## No Setup Fee

The core proposition should avoid a separate setup fee.

The website and initial configuration are positioned as included with the platform's promotional service.

## Revenue

Revenue is generated from eligible delivered promotional interactions.

Examples:
- clicks
- calls
- qualified leads

The exact monetization model must be validated.

## Main Costs

- Google Ads
- Stripe fees
- AI
- Voice
- Avatar
- Hosting
- Storage
- Infrastructure
- Growth
- Support
- Operations

## Main Business Question

Can the platform acquire traffic for less than the value generated by that traffic?
