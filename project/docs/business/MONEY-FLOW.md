# Money Flow

## Payment

Business pays through Stripe.

Example:

250 RON payment
→ Client Credit = 250 RON

Revenue is not automatically equal to 250 RON.

## Credit Consumption

When promotional services are delivered, client credit decreases.

Example:

250 RON → 245 RON

Revenue recognized for the delivered service: 5 RON.

## Traffic Cost

Google Ads creates an external traffic cost.

Example:

Google Cost = 3 RON
Internal Revenue = 5 RON
Contribution before other costs = 2 RON

## Financial Separation

The system must separately track:
- Stripe cash
- client credit
- recognized revenue
- Google cost accrued
- Google cost paid
- Stripe fees
- refunds
- operating expenses
- taxes
- available cash

## Important Principle

Client credit is not profit.

Unconsumed client credit represents an obligation to provide the purchased service or otherwise handle the balance according to applicable commercial and legal rules.
