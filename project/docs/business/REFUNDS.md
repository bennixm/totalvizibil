# Refunds

## Principle

Refund handling must be transparent and compliant with applicable Romanian and EU law.

## Important Separation

The platform must distinguish:
1. Unconsumed promotional credit
2. Services already delivered
3. Costs already incurred
4. Refundable amounts

## Example

Initial credit: 250 RON

Consumed: 80 RON

Remaining: 170 RON

The system must track the 170 RON separately from recognized revenue.

## Important

Do not artificially consume client credit to avoid refunds.

Do not design refund rules solely around recovering internal AI costs.

Final refund policy must be reviewed legally before launch.
