# MVP

## Goal

Validate that businesses are willing to activate promotional credit and that the platform can generate profitable traffic and leads.

## MVP Features

### Business
- Account creation
- Business profile
- Services
- Locations
- AI onboarding
- Website template
- Website preview
- Website publishing
- Promotional credit
- Stripe payment
- Basic campaign
- Basic analytics

### Customer
- Classic search
- Business results
- Business profile
- Contact action
- Click tracking

### Platform
- Basic ranking
- Google Ads integration
- Traffic tracking
- Credit ledger
- Revenue tracking
- Cost tracking
- Admin dashboard

## AI MVP
- Voice input
- Speech-to-text
- LLM
- Text-to-speech
- Basic avatar
- Business information extraction
- Website content generation

## NOT MVP
- Photorealistic avatar
- Fully autonomous AI marketing
- Advanced AI search
- Advanced machine-learning ranking
- Mobile application
- Full accounting automation
- Complex recommendation engine
- Large-scale automation

## MVP Success Criteria

The MVP must answer:
1. Do businesses activate?
2. How much credit do they deposit?
3. How much credit do they consume?
4. How much does traffic cost?
5. How much revenue does traffic generate?
6. Is the contribution margin positive?
7. Do businesses return and reload credit?
